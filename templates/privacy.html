Privacy policy

{{ app_name }} never stores your text or sends it anywhere{% if analytics_enabled %}. Google Analytics counts visits, but never sees your text{% else %}. It doesn't track you and doesn't use cookies{% endif %}.

Your text

With JavaScript turned on, as it is in almost every browser, all cleaning happens on your own device. Your text is never sent to our servers.

If JavaScript is turned off, the text is sent to our server only to be cleaned and returned to you. It is not saved, logged or shared, and it is gone as soon as the page is sent back.

Files you open or drop into {{ app_name }} are read by your browser on your device. They are never uploaded.

Paraphrase with AI

The optional AI paraphraser runs entirely on your device. When you first use it, your browser downloads the AI model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and the code that runs it from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), and keeps them in its cache. Those services see that a download happened, like any website visit, but never your text: it is processed on your device and never sent anywhere.

Cookies and analytics

{% if analytics_enabled %}

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which tools are popular. It records pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and sets cookies to tell visits apart. It never receives the text you clean. Google processes this data under its privacy policy, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or a content blocker.

{{ app_name }} itself sets no cookies and uses no advertising.

{% else %}

{{ app_name }} sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

{% endif %}

Your color theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider records basic technical information about requests, such as the page address, time and IP address, to keep the service secure and running. These logs never contain the text you clean.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
