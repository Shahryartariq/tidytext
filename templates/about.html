About {{ app_name }}

{{ app_name }} fixes the mess text picks up when you copy it from one place to another.

Why it exists

Text copied from a PDF breaks at the end of every printed line. Text from a web page brings hidden characters and odd spaces. Text typed with caps lock on has to be retyped. {{ app_name }} fixes each of these in a few clicks, without installing anything or signing up.

There are {{ tools|length }} tools, from fixing PDF text to sorting lists and counting words. They all use the same editor, so you can combine them: join broken lines, remove extra spaces and fix the letter case in one go.

What happens to your text

Nothing leaves your browser. Your text is cleaned on your device as you type, and there are no accounts{% if not analytics_enabled %}, cookies or trackers{% endif %}. The counts under each box show exactly how many characters, words and lines changed. The privacy page has the details.
